4. What does this tell us?

Stocks with stronger average CAGR during Democratic administrations:

  • AMZN: Slightly higher Democratic-period average, with particularly strong growth during the Obama years.
  • HD: Much stronger average during Democratic periods, especially under Obama.
  • MCD: Relatively similar averages, with a modest Democratic-period advantage.
  • BKNG: Strong Democratic-period average, driven by substantial gains during Obama and Biden.

Stock with stronger average CAGR during Republican administrations:

  • TSLA: Strong Republican-period average, heavily influenced by its extraordinary 2020 performance during Trump's first term.

Overall interpretation

  • Democratic administrations had the higher average CAGR for 4 out of 5 stocks in this group.
  • Tesla was the exception, with a much higher Republican-period average.
  • Amazon's results were relatively close between the two groups.
  • Booking and Home Depot showed particularly large differences in their historical period averages.

These results should not be interpreted as a political investment strategy. Interest rates, economic cycles, company-specific execution, technology adoption, valuations and market conditions can have much greater influence on individual stock returns than the party in power.

Methodology reminder: These are historical price-return comparisons, not dividend-reinvested total returns. The administration averages are simple averages of each available administration's CAGR, not portfolio returns. For a more complete investment comparison, the next improvement would be to compare each stock against the S&P 500 over the same periods.
