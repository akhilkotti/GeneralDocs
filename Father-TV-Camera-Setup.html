Hardware List

Communication

  • 43" Samsung Smart TV
  • Dedicated Android phone (Samsung Galaxy A-series or similar)

Monitoring

  • 3 × TP-Link Tapo C220
  • 3 × 256 GB microSD cards

Network

  • Fiber broadband
  • Dual-band Wi-Fi router

Power Backup

  • Router UPS
  • Optional larger UPS for TV

Step 1: Install Broadband

Choose:

  • JioFiber
  • Airtel Xstream Fiber

Recommended plan:

  • 100 Mbps or higher

Router location:

  • Central location in the house.

Step 2: Install Cameras

Bedroom Camera

Mount:

  • 7–8 feet high
  • Corner of room

Enable:

  • Person Detection
  • Motion Detection
  • Night Vision

Staircase Camera

Mount:

  • Top landing preferred

Reason:

  • One camera can see the entire staircase.

Bathroom Camera

Mount:

  • Outside bathroom
  • Facing hallway

Purpose:

  • Know when your father enters/exits.
  • Preserve privacy.

Step 3: Configure Tapo App

Install Tapo app on your phone.

Add all three cameras.

Name them:

Father Bedroom
Staircase
Bathroom

Enable:

✅ Motion Detection

✅ Person Detection

✅ Push Notifications

✅ Continuous Recording

Insert:

  • 256GB SD card in each camera

Step 4: Install TV

Mount TV opposite:

  • Bed
  • Recliner
  • Favorite chair

Viewing distance:

  • 6–10 feet

Step 5: Dedicated Android Phone

Purchase a dedicated phone.

Suggestions:

  • Samsung Galaxy A16
  • Samsung Galaxy A26
  • Any reliable Android phone with good front camera

Purpose:

  • Video communication endpoint

Leave it:

  • Permanently connected to charger
  • Permanently connected to Wi-Fi

Step 6: Mount Phone Near TV

Use a wall mount.

      Phone
        ▲
        │
 ┌──────────────┐
 │    43" TV    │
 └──────────────┘

Position:

  • Front camera facing bed/chair.

Step 7: Auto-Answer Configuration

This is the most important step.

For the dedicated phone:

  1. Create a separate Google account.
  2. Add only family contacts.
  3. Enable accessibility-based auto-answer solution (varies by device and app).
  4. Restrict incoming calls to approved contacts.

Test thoroughly before deployment.

Step 8: Video Display on TV

Option A (Recommended)

Keep phone above TV.

Your father looks toward TV and phone.

Simple and reliable.

Option B

Use Samsung Smart View / casting.

Phone screen mirrors to TV.

When you call:

  • Video appears on TV.
  • Phone camera captures your father.

Step 10: Remote Monitoring

From your phone:

  • Caretaker assisting

Check Staircase

Ensure safe movement.

Check Bathroom

Verify entry/exit activity.

Talk Through Cameras

Speak instantly.

Daily Workflow

Monitoring

Open Tapo app anytime:

  • Bedroom
  • Staircase
  • Bathroom entrance

Communication

Initiate video call from your phone.

Dedicated phone receives the call.

Video appears on the TV (if mirroring is configured).

Recording

All three cameras continuously record.

You can review footage later if needed.
