Steps to Check e-PAN Status / Download e-PAN

Step 1:

Click the below link to verify the e-PAN status:

https://eportal.incometax.gov.in/iec/foservices/#/pre-login/instant-e-pan

Step 2:

Click on the "Continue" link under Check Status / Download e-PAN.

Step 3:

Enter the Aadhaar number in the provided box and click on the "Continue" button.

You will receive an OTP on the mobile number registered with Aadhaar.

Step 4:

Enter the received OTP and click on the "Continue" button.

Step 5:

On the next page, you will be able to see the status of the e-PAN application.

Note: Ensure that the mobile number linked with Aadhaar is active to receive the OTP successfully.
